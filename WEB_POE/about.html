Our Story

Passion, community, and the perfect cup of coffee.

From a Dream to a Community Hub

Bloom & Bean was founded in 2018 by a husband-and-wife team — a barista and a pastry chef — who shared a vision of creating a space where great coffee, delicious food, and genuine human connection come together.

Nestled in the heart of Stellenbosch, our café has grown from a small coffee shop with a few tables into a beloved local institution. We pride ourselves on sourcing coffee beans directly from ethical farms in the region and using only the freshest, locally-sourced ingredients in everything we make.

Whether you're here to work, catch up with friends, or simply enjoy a moment of peace in our plant-filled space — you are always welcome at Bloom & Bean.
